# Avengers: Doomsday — Watch Order

Release: **December 18, 2026** (some markets, such as the UK, get it on December 16). Researched 2026-11-24, the day after the final trailer. Rechecked the same day against four more guides.

Install it in Nuvio: `https://4si8.github.io/road-to-doomsday/manifest.json`

## Sources

This list combines five guides and checks them against what the final trailer shows. The "Picked by" column uses these codes:

- **D+**: Marvel's official Disney+ "Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday" collection (15 titles)
- **RT**: Rotten Tomatoes, "The Essential Marvel Movies to Watch Before Avengers: Doomsday" (Nov 13, 2026). It has the Disney+ titles plus VisionQuest.
- **MW**: marvelwatchlist.com/before-doomsday. Grades titles as **E**ssential, **R**ecommended or **O**ptional.
- **9M**: 9to5Marvel, "What to watch before Avengers: Doomsday" (updated Nov 23, 2026). Grades titles as **E**ssential (9 titles), **R**ecommended or **O**ptional.
- **MM**: Murphy's Multiverse, "The Ultimate List" (Oct 21, 2026). No tiers.

Two other guides were checked but don't get a code:

- **roadtodoomsday.com** includes almost everything (70 stops, about 230 hours), so it doesn't help tell titles apart.
- **JustWatch** couldn't be fully read. The part that was readable calls X-Men: Days of Future Past essential.

## Tiers

- ⭐ **Essential**: needed to follow the plot, or the latest appearance of a confirmed character
- ➕ **Recommended**: sets up a confirmed character, or at least three guides include it
- ◽ **Optional**: deep cut

## Watch order

The list is in **release order**. That's the easiest way to follow three continuities at once: Fox's X-Men, the MCU and the Fantastic Four's Earth-828. MW uses story order instead, which mainly means moving Captain America: The First Avenger ahead of X-Men. Either order works.

## If you're short on time (about 21 hours)

1. X2
2. Avengers: Endgame Encore, including the new end-credit scenes
3. Loki Season 2
4. Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness
5. Deadpool & Wolverine
6. Captain America: Brave New World
7. Thunderbolts*
8. The Fantastic Four: First Steps
9. VisionQuest Episode 8 and its post-credits scene

Murphy's Multiverse's "Key Three" (Endgame Encore, Loki, Multiverse of Madness) is all inside this list. So are the four titles every guide treats as required: Endgame, Loki, Multiverse of Madness and Deadpool & Wolverine.

## Totals

- Official Disney+ 15: about 44 hours
- All ⭐ titles: about 52 hours or more (VisionQuest's runtime wasn't available)
- Everything on this list: about 92 hours

## What the sources disagree on

- **Spider-Man: Brand New Day**: 9to5Marvel rates it essential, MW rates it optional, and Murphy's leaves it out. Spider-Man isn't confirmed for Doomsday, so it stays ➕.
- **Thor: Love and Thunder**: MW rates it essential and 9to5Marvel rates it optional. Disney+ and RT leave out every Thor film. It's ⭐ here because Thor is confirmed.
- **VisionQuest**: RT, MW and Murphy's include it because of the finale tease. Its showrunner says the timing with Doomsday is "a coincidence," and GamesRadar calls the link a stretch.
- **X-Men: Days of Future Past**: JustWatch calls it essential. MW rates it recommended, 9to5Marvel rates it optional, and Disney+ leaves it out.
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3: no confirmed tie to Doomsday, and MW excludes it.
  - Logan and Eternals: optional in every guide that lists them.
  - Moon Knight and Secret Invasion: 9to5Marvel says they're skippable.
  - What If...? Season 1 Episode 4: only roadtodoomsday.com requires it.
  - The Netflix and Daredevil street-level shows: no Doomsday tie.
  - Ms. Marvel: Murphy's lists it under "younger Avengers," but Kamala isn't confirmed.

## Characters confirmed or seen in trailers (as of the Nov 23, 2026 final trailer)

- **Avengers:** Sam Wilson / Captain America, Thor, Ant-Man and Cassie Lang, Shang-Chi, Kate Bishop, Steve Rogers (with Peggy and their son)
- **Wakanda / Talokan:** Shuri, Talokan
- **Thunderbolts / New Avengers:** Yelena, Bucky, US Agent, Red Guardian, Sentry
- **Fantastic Four:** Reed, Sue, Johnny, Ben
- **X-Men:** Xavier, Magneto, Cyclops, Mystique, Nightcrawler, Beast, Gambit
- **Villain:** Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.). His motive: the heroes have "lived stolen lives"
- **Loki** is in the cast but barely shown in the trailers
- **Scarlet Witch** is teased by VisionQuest but not confirmed
