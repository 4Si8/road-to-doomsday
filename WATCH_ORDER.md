# Avengers: Doomsday — Watch Order

Release: **December 18, 2026** (some markets, such as the UK, get it on December 16). Researched 2026-11-24, the day after the final trailer.

This list combines three sources and checks them against what the final trailer shows:

- **D+**: Marvel's official Disney+ "Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday" collection (15 titles)
- **RT**: Rotten Tomatoes, "The Essential Marvel Movies to Watch Before Avengers: Doomsday" (Nov 13, 2026). It has the Disney+ titles plus VisionQuest.
- **MW**: marvelwatchlist.com/before-doomsday, which grades titles as **E**ssential, **R**ecommended or **O**ptional

Tiers in this list:

- ⭐ **Essential**: needed to follow the plot
- ➕ **Recommended**: sets up a character who is confirmed or appears in a trailer
- ◽ **Optional**: deep cut

The list is in **release order**. That's the easiest way to follow three continuities at once: Fox's X-Men, the MCU and the Fantastic Four's Earth-828. MW uses story order instead, which mainly means moving Captain America: The First Avenger ahead of X-Men. Either order works.

## Totals

- Official Disney+ 15: about 44 hours
- All ⭐ titles: about 50 hours or more (VisionQuest's runtime wasn't available)
- Everything on this list: about 85 hours

## What the sources disagree on

- **Thor: Love and Thunder**: MW grades it Essential. Disney+ and RT leave out every Thor film. It's ⭐ here because Thor is confirmed.
- **VisionQuest**: only RT includes it, because of the finale tease. Its showrunner says the timing with Doomsday is "a coincidence," and GamesRadar calls the link a stretch.
- **X-Men: Days of Future Past**: not on the official list. Fans on Reddit question that, and MW grades it Recommended.
- **Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3**: MW deliberately excludes it. Nothing ties it to Doomsday.

## Characters confirmed or seen in trailers (as of the Nov 23, 2026 final trailer)

- **Avengers:** Sam Wilson / Captain America, Thor, Ant-Man and Cassie Lang, Shang-Chi, Kate Bishop, Steve Rogers (with Peggy and their son)
- **Wakanda / Talokan:** Shuri, Talokan
- **Thunderbolts / New Avengers:** Yelena, Bucky, US Agent, Red Guardian, Sentry
- **Fantastic Four:** Reed, Sue, Johnny, Ben
- **X-Men:** Xavier, Magneto, Cyclops, Mystique, Nightcrawler, Beast, Gambit
- **Villain:** Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.). His motive: the heroes have "lived stolen lives"
- **Loki** is in the cast but barely shown in the trailers
- **Scarlet Witch** is teased by VisionQuest but not confirmed
